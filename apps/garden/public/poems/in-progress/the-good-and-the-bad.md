# The Good & The Bad

> Before the gate

In everything you hear or read
Know you are being given seeds

Some are good and some are bad
By fruit you know what you had

If they send out poison shoots
Rip them up right by the roots

If you find fruit that's sweet
Clear the weeds so you can eat

Your plants need the sun above
As well as water and some love

Down each path your feet tread
Blossom flowering in your head

Approach slowly but never late
As you come to the garden gate


---

> At the gate


A lock is there upon the doors
Beyond echoing infinite shores

Will you pass to that far dock
To stand at once upon the rock

Below though many fathoms deep
A place where some evils sleep

So do not linger and tarry not
Here child is your garden plot

Do not attend to what's behind
Take up peace with silent mind
